Summary

Bijli Hisaab works without an account. Your meters, readings, bills and appliances are stored only on your phone. We do not run any server that collects your data, we show no ads, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

What stays on your phone

  • Meters (name, electricity company, meter type, reference number), meter readings, bills, appliances and settings, kept in a local database on the device.
  • Backup files you create, which are saved or shared only where you choose (for example Google Drive or WhatsApp).

When data leaves your phone

  • Electricity rates: the app downloads the latest rates file from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). Nothing about you is sent.
  • Bill online: when you use it, your bill reference number is sent to your electricity company's official bill website (bill.pitc.com.pk) to fetch your bill. Only the bill figures are saved in the app, never your name or address.
  • Assistant: simple questions are answered on your phone. Questions it cannot answer, together with your bill figures (units, amounts, dates), are sent to a free AI service, Pollinations (text.pollinations.ai), to get a reply. Web searches made by the assistant are sent to DuckDuckGo. Your name, address and reference number are never sent.
  • Voice input: when you tap the microphone, speech is turned into text by your phone's speech service (usually Google), under that service's own privacy policy.

All connections use HTTPS.

Camera and photos

Bill and meter photos are read on the device using Google ML Kit. The app does not upload or keep the photos; only the numbers you confirm are saved.

Notifications

Reminders and slab alerts are created and scheduled on your phone. No push server is used.

Optional anonymous export

If you turn it on, you can export CSV files (city, company, units, bills, app events) and share them yourself. They contain a random ID, never your name, phone number, reference number or address.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan bills and meter readings.
  • Microphone: only when you use voice input.
  • Notifications, alarms, run at startup: to show reminders on time and keep them after a restart.
  • Internet: for rates, Bill online and the assistant.

Children

The app is meant for adults who pay electricity bills and is not directed at children under 13.

Deleting your data

Tools → Reset app deletes all data from your phone. Uninstalling the app also deletes it. Because we keep no copy, there is nothing else to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

  • بجلی کے ریٹ: ایپ تازہ ریٹ GitHub سے ڈاؤن لوڈ کرتی ہے۔ آپ کے بارے میں کچھ نہیں بھیجا جاتا۔

  • اسسٹنٹ: آسان سوالوں کا جواب فون پر ہی ملتا ہے۔ باقی سوال آپ کے بل کے اعداد کے ساتھ ایک مفت AI سروس (Pollinations) کو بھیجے جاتے ہیں، اور ویب سرچ DuckDuckGo پر ہوتی ہے۔ نام، پتہ اور ریفرنس نمبر کبھی نہیں بھیجے جاتے۔
