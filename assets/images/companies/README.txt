Optional company logos.

Put a square PNG (at least 256x256, transparent or white background) here
named after the company code in lowercase, with "-" replaced by "_":

  lesco.png  iesco.png  fesco.png  gepco.png  mepco.png  pesco.png
  hazeco.png tesco.png  hesco.png  sepco.png  qesco.png  k_electric.png
  ajk_ed.png gb_wpd.png

The app shows the logo automatically wherever the company appears
(meter form, company picker, More screen). Without a file it shows a
clean coloured badge with the company code.

Logos are trademarks of each company. Use them only with permission and
say in your Play Store listing that the app is not affiliated with them —
Google Play can reject apps that look like they come from a utility.
